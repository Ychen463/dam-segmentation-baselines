# Conference Guidelines

## Target: CVPR 2026 (or similar top-tier computer vision venue)

### Format
- Double-column format (IEEE style)
- Main paper: 8 pages maximum (excluding references)
- References: unlimited pages
- Supplementary material: optional, separate PDF

### Required Sections
- Abstract (< 200 words)
- Introduction
- Related Work
- Method
- Experiments
- Conclusion
- References

### Formatting Rules
- Paper size: US Letter (8.5 × 11 inches)
- Font: Times Roman, 10pt for body text
- Column width: 3.25 inches with 0.375 inch gutter
- Margins: 1 inch top, 0.75 inch sides, 1 inch bottom
- Figures and tables may span both columns
- All figures must be referenced in text
- Citations: numbered, in order of appearance

### Review Criteria
- Novelty and significance of contribution
- Technical soundness
- Experimental validation (comparison with SOTA, ablation study)
- Clarity of presentation
- Reproducibility

### Language
- English
- Write in third person or first person plural ("we")
